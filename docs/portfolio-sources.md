## Endemic garden

Alexis requested variety based on endemic flowers on 16 October 2026. The five taxa below have native ranges confined to Mexico in the primary botanical sources. Their silhouettes, growth forms and planting positions inform original procedural models. Colors and scale are interpreted for the cosmic palette; the planting is a speculative garden, not a reconstruction of historical Tenochtitlan or a botanical identification illustration.
