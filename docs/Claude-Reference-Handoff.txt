AXO's direction: a small robotic axolotl walks in profile along a circular stone causeway inspired by Tenochtitlan. The lake town rotates beneath him as the visitor scrolls. Each building represents a project. AXO turns toward the building on arrival. The background is a cosmos with pink stellar clouds. Palette: #231942, #5e548e, #9f86c0, #be95c4, #e0b1cb. No bicycle. The site needs readable case studies, clear navigation, natural motion, and responsive stars, flowers, and flowing water.
